Your diet

Your diet and restrictions apply to every recommendation. Vegan rules and explicit restrictions always take priority.

Restrictions

Foods JavaanFitness must never recommend. Separate entries with commas or new lines, up to 32 per field.

Forbidden foods match exact meal names. Use forbidden ingredients to exclude an ingredient from every dish. Foods with insufficient ingredient information may be excluded.

Preferences

Foods and styles JavaanFitness should prefer when suitable. Usually avoided foods remain eligible. Separate entries with commas or new lines.

Recommendation timing

Local time in your saved timezone. Default: 23:30.
